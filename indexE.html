FAQ & How It Works

Why no contact saving?

This tool utilizes WhatsApp's official API deep-link (`wa.me`) protocol to directly open chats without accessing or modifying your device address book.

Is my data private?

100% Secure! All records and message drafts remain strictly inside your local browser (LocalStorage). No backend servers collect your data.

How to use on Desktop?

Clicking on desktop opens WhatsApp Web or Desktop App automatically. You can also generate a QR Code to scan with your phone camera.
